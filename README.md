# Low-Cost Lightweight Milk Chilling Can

## About the Project

This project focuses on the development of a low-cost and lightweight milk chilling can for small-scale dairy farmers.

The aim is to help maintain milk quality during milk collection and transportation.

## Problem

Milk quality can decrease when freshly collected milk remains at ambient temperature for a long time, especially in areas with limited access to chilling facilities.

## Proposed Solution

We are developing a lightweight and affordable milk chilling can designed for small-scale dairy farmers by peltier thermoelectric cooling module.temperature sensor will monitot the milk temp. suitable insulation is also used
